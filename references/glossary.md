# Plain-words glossary

Use these when the person says "explain". One or two sentences, then back to the task.

- **Agent:** an AI set up to do one job the same way each time, with its own instructions and limits.
- **Playbook (skill):** a saved set of instructions your assistant follows when a matching request comes up.
- **Helper (subagent, custom agent):** a specialist your assistant hands a job to. It has its own instructions, its own tool limits, and can use a different model.
- **Trigger:** what starts the agent without you asking: a time (schedule) or something happening (an event).
- **Description:** the one-line summary your assistant reads to decide whether to use a playbook or helper.
- **Connector (MCP server):** a link that lets the AI read or act in another app, such as email or a calendar.
- **Permission mode / sandbox:** the app's own lock on what the AI can do without asking. Stronger than an instruction.
- **Model:** which AI brain runs the agent. Bigger ones reason better and cost more.
- **Effort:** how long the model thinks before answering. More effort is slower and costs more.
- **Local vs cloud:** local runs on your computer and can see your files, but needs the computer on. Cloud runs on the provider's servers with the computer off, but only reaches what you connect.
- **Frontmatter:** the few settings lines at the top of an agent or skill file, between two lines of dashes.
- **Terminal:** a text window where you type commands. Only needed for a few setups; the builder will say exactly what to type.
- **Dry run:** trying the agent on a sample, with nothing sent or deleted, to see if it works.
